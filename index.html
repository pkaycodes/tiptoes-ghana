Ask us on WhatsApp
What can Tiptoes source for me?

Heels, bags, perfumes, clothes and fashion accessories from UK and US brands. If it’s fashion-related and you can send us a link or screenshot, ask us — we’ll do our best to source it.

How do preorders work?

Send us the item you want. We confirm the price, size, colour and timeline, then share the payment details. Once your order is confirmed, we place it with the retailer and keep you updated until it reaches Ghana.

How long does delivery take?

Most preorders take around 7–14 business days after confirmation. Timelines can vary depending on the retailer, dispatch times and customs. We’ll always confirm the expected window before you pay.

Do you deliver outside Accra?

Yes — nationwide delivery across Ghana is available. Delivery fees are confirmed based on your location when you place your order.

How do I pay?

Payment details are shared securely on WhatsApp. A deposit confirms your preorder, and the balance is settled before delivery. Exact payment options are confirmed when you order.

Are the items authentic?

We source from trusted UK and US retailers and check your order before it ships. Tiptoes is an independent personal shopping service and is not affiliated with the brands featured.

What if I’m not sure about sizing?

Send us your usual size and any fit notes. We’ll help you choose based on the retailer’s size guide. Because items are sourced to order, returns are handled case by case — just message us before ordering if you’re unsure.
